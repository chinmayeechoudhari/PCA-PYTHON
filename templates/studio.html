01
Anomaly Detection
Flag images that deviate from the learned normal distribution

The model learns what "normal" looks like from training images. Unusual images reconstruct poorly — their MSE is high. Images above the threshold are flagged as anomalies. Applied in quality control, fraud detection, and medical screening.

Threshold = mean_train_error × multiplier. Higher → fewer anomalies flagged.

Model will score it against the learned training distribution.
